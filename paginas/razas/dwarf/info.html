Raza: Dwarf

Velocidad: 25 pies

Tamaño: Dwarves stand between 4 and 5 feet tall and average about 150 pounds. Your size is Medium.

Alineamiento: Most dwarves are lawful, believing firmly in the benefits of a well-ordered society. They tend toward good as well, with a strong sense of fair play and a belief that everyone deserves to share in the benefits of a just order.

Bonos de Característica

Rasgos Raciales

  • Dwarven Resilience
  • Stonecunning
  • Dwarven Combat Training
  • Tool Proficiency
