Raza: Tiefling

Velocidad: 30 pies

Tamaño: Tieflings are about the same size and build as humans. Your size is Medium.

Alineamiento: Tieflings might not have an innate tendency toward evil, but many of them end up there. Evil or not, an independent nature inclines many tieflings toward a chaotic alignment.

Bonos de Característica

  • INT +1
  • CHA +2

Rasgos Raciales

  • Darkvision
  • Hellish Resistance
  • Infernal Legacy
