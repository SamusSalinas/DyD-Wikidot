Raza: Half-Elf

Velocidad: 30 pies

Tamaño: Half-elves are about the same size as humans, ranging from 5 to 6 feet tall. Your size is Medium.

Alineamiento: Half-elves share the chaotic bent of their elven heritage. They value both personal freedom and creative expression, demonstrating neither love of leaders nor desire for followers. They chafe at rules, resent others' demands, and sometimes prove unreliable, or at least unpredictable.
