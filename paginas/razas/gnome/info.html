Raza: Gnome

Velocidad: 25 pies

Tamaño: Gnomes are between 3 and 4 feet tall and average about 40 pounds. Your size is Small.

Alineamiento: Gnomes are most often good. Those who tend toward law are sages, engineers, researchers, scholars, investigators, or inventors. Those who tend toward chaos are minstrels, tricksters, wanderers, or fanciful jewelers. Gnomes are good-hearted, and even the tricksters among them are more playful than vicious.

Bonos de Característica

  • INT +2

Rasgos Raciales

  • Darkvision
  • Gnome Cunning

Subrazas:

  • Rock Gnome
